Bennett Reloaded Uploader

A private publishing tool for the YouTube channel Bennett Reloaded.

Bennett makes short animated films in which every frame is rendered by code. Bennett Reloaded Uploader is the small script he runs on his own computer to put each finished film on his channel. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the video as private and to fill in its title, description, tags and thumbnail. Bennett then reviews it in YouTube Studio and decides when it goes public.

It is not a public service. It has no sign-up and no other users, and it only ever acts on Bennett's own channel.
