Privacy policy

Bennett Reloaded Uploader. Last updated 16 October 2026.

Bennett Reloaded Uploader is a private tool that uploads Bennett's own animated films to his YouTube channel, Bennett Reloaded. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

With the account holder's consent, it uses two Google permissions. One uploads videos to the signed-in YouTube channel. The other reads that channel's own information: its ID and name, and the status of the videos it uploaded. It does not read viewers' data, comments, analytics or any other account.

How it uses that access

It uploads a finished film with its title, description, tags and thumbnail, always as a private video. It then checks that the upload went to the right channel and has finished processing.

What it stores

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the computer where the tool runs. Nothing is sent anywhere except to Google's own servers. There is no database and no analytics.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Removing access

The account holder can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page. Deleting the token from the computer also ends its access.

Contact

Through the Bennett Reloaded channel.
